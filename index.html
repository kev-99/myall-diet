🚨 CRITICAL DIGESTION NOTE (Next 3 Days):
STRICTLY AVOID: Mushrooms, raw vegetables, broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, beans, lentils, and berries with seeds. They cause severe water retention, gas, and abdominal bloating while locked in a kayak cockpit at 145 BPM.

2. Race Day Hourly Timeline (Saturday - 11:00 AM Start)

Time Action Item & Fueling Strategy
8:00 AM
(3 hours prior)		 Pre-Race Breakfast & Routine: Eat quick oats with honey, or 2 Saladas/Sakatas with a thin scrape of Vegemite.
☕ Caffeine: Enjoy your morning coffee or tea here. STRICTLY use Skim Milk this morning to ensure your stomach is completely light, empty, and settled. Drink 300ml–500ml of water or weak Staminade over the next hour.
10:00 AM
(1 hour prior)		 Fluid Lockdown: Stop drinking large volumes of fluid so your bladder can completely empty before you lock into the cockpit. Take small comfort sips of water only if your mouth feels dry.
10:45 AM
(15 mins prior)		 Pre-Load Sugar: Take your 1st Energy Gel with a small splash of plain water from your backup floor bottle to kickstart blood glucose levels.
11:00 AM RACE START: Settle cleanly into your 145 BPM target pacing rhythm.
11:00 - 11:45 AM
(First 45 mins)		 Sip consistently from your 1L Staminade bladder (aiming to clear roughly 1/3 of the volume).
11:45 AM
(45 min mark)		 2nd Energy Gel: Consume and follow with a quick swallow of plain water from your floor bottle to assist rapid absorption.
11:45 - 12:30 PM
(45m to 1.5h)		 Continue sipping from the Staminade bladder. In the 25°C sun, start tapping into your plain water floor bottle to clear any sticky sweetness from your mouth and stay cool.
12:30 PM
(1.5 hour mark)		 3rd Energy Gel: Your vital insurance policy to keep your upper body firing and prevent "bonking" over the final hour of work.
12:30 - 1:30 PM
(Final stretch)		 Finish off the Staminade bladder and lean heavily on your 500ml water floor bottle to stay hydrated, cool, and strong to the line.
1:30 PM ESTIMATED FINISH: 2.5 hours complete. Begin immediate recovery with clean fluids and proteins.

3. Cockpit Gear Setup Check

  • 1L Bladder: Mixed to standard-strength Staminade (~57g carbs total).
  • 500ml Floor Bottle: Plain water (brings total race fluid to a safe 1.5L for 25°C heat).
  • 3 Energy Gels: Taped securely to your spray deck or tucked in your PFD pocket for quick one-handed access.
